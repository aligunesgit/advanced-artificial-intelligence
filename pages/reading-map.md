# D2L reading map

Primary source: [Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/index.html), online edition 1.0.3, checked 15 October 2026. Use the PyTorch track. Section numbers refer to this edition; use section titles if the live site changes.

Start with the [AI introductory sequence](../intro/what-is-ai.md). It provides broader context before the D2L technical track. Review for the technical modules: selected material from chapters 2–6. Optional mathematics support: chapter 22. Each module assigns a selection, not an entire chapter unless explicitly stated.

| Week | Module | Assigned sections and optional extensions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [M1: Deep Learning Foundations and Research Practice](../m1_foundations/README.md) | [3.6–3.7, 4.6–4.7, 5.3–5.6; review chapters 2–6 as needed.](https://d2l.ai/chapter_multilayer-perceptrons/index.html) |
| 2 | [M2: Optimization and Hyperparameter Search](../m2_optimization/README.md) | [12.1–12.6, 12.10–12.11; 19.1 and 19.3–19.5.](https://d2l.ai/chapter_optimization/index.html) |
| 3 | [M3: Convolutional Architectures and Residual Learning](../m3_cnn/README.md) | [7.1–7.6; 8.5–8.6. Other architectures in chapter 8 are optional comparisons.](https://d2l.ai/chapter_convolutional-neural-networks/index.html) |
| 4 | [M4: Transfer Learning and Computer Vision](../m4_transfer_learning/README.md) | [14.1–14.2 required; 14.3 and 14.9–14.11 provide an optional task survey.](https://d2l.ai/chapter_computer-vision/index.html) |
| 5 | [M5: Sequence Models and Recurrent Networks](../m5_sequence_models/README.md) | [9.1–9.7; 10.1–10.2 and 10.6–10.7.](https://d2l.ai/chapter_recurrent-neural-networks/index.html) |

Weeks 6 and 12 are project sprints. Chapters 13 and 21 are optional project resources; large-scale pretraining and multi-GPU execution are not required. Causal inference, symbolic planning, VAE, and diffusion topics from the initial draft are outside this D2L-based core.

The derivations, experiment controls, uncertainty reporting, and discussion prompts in the course briefs are instructor-designed extensions. They should not be attributed to the textbook as verbatim assignments.
